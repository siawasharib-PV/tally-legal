Privacy Policy - Tally Money Manager

Effective date: 13 August 2026

Tally Money Manager ("Tally") is provided by Pada Ventures Pty Ltd ("we", "our", or "us"). This policy explains the information Tally collects, how it is used, when it may be shared, and the choices available to users.

1. Information Tally may collect

The information collected depends on the features you use:

  • Account information: email address and an internal user identifier if you create an account. Account creation is optional because Tally can be used in guest mode.
  • Financial records you enter: transactions, amounts, dates, categories, descriptions, budgets, workspaces, recurring transaction settings, travel-log entries, reminders, and related user-generated content.
  • Receipt information: receipt attachment details and locally selected files used with transactions. Tally does not use receipt images for advertising.
  • Subscription information: product, plan, entitlement, and purchase-status information required to provide and restore Premium access. Payment card details are processed by Apple or Google and are not received by Tally.
  • Analytics and app activity: app interactions, feature usage, app-open events, diagnostics, performance information, and related technical events processed through Firebase services.
  • Advertising information for Free users: device or advertising identifiers, approximate location derived from an IP address, consent choices, app activity, diagnostics, performance information, and advertising interactions processed by Google AdMob.

2. How information is used

  • Provide account access, optional cloud sync, financial tracking, budgets, reminders, reports, and other app functionality.
  • Provide, verify, and restore Premium subscriptions.
  • Measure app usage, diagnose faults, improve reliability, and understand feature performance.
  • Display and measure advertising for Free users and prevent advertising fraud or abuse.
  • Protect accounts, comply with legal obligations, respond to support requests, and enforce our terms.

3. Advertising and analytics

Tally uses Firebase Analytics to understand app usage and performance. Tally may display advertising supplied by Google AdMob to users of the Free version. Google may process device identifiers, approximate location derived from an IP address, app interactions, diagnostics, performance data, consent choices, and ad interactions for advertising, analytics, measurement, security, and fraud prevention. Premium users are not shown in-app ads while their subscription is active.

Where required, Tally presents advertising privacy choices through Google's consent platform. The availability of personalised advertising depends on the user's region, consent, device settings, and Google policies.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate Tally, including hosting and database providers, Apple and Google for purchases, Firebase for analytics and technical services, and Google AdMob for advertising in the Free version. These providers process information under their own terms, privacy policies, and applicable data-processing arrangements.

5. Guest mode and optional account sync

You may use Tally without creating an account. Guest-mode financial records are stored locally on the device unless a feature clearly states otherwise. If you create an account and use sync features, account information and the financial records you choose to sync are transmitted securely to Tally's backend so they can be made available to your account.

6. Data retention

Account and synced app data is retained while the account is active and as reasonably necessary to provide Tally. Limited records may be retained where required for security, fraud prevention, legal obligations, dispute resolution, or financial recordkeeping. Local guest data remains on the device until it is deleted through the app, app storage is cleared, or the app is removed.

7. Account and data deletion

You can permanently delete a Tally account and its associated synced data directly in the app:

  1. Open Tally Money Manager and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Select Delete account and complete the confirmation steps.

If you cannot access the app, email hello@pada.com.au from the email address associated with the account and request deletion.

Account deletion removes the account and associated synced transactions, categories, budgets, workspaces, recurring entries, receipt records, reminders, travel-log entries, and related account data from active systems, subject to the limited retention reasons described above. App-store purchase records are managed separately by Apple or Google under their policies.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, including encrypted network connections, to protect information. No transmission or storage method is completely secure, but we regularly review and improve our safeguards.

9. Children

Tally is not intended for children under 13 or the applicable minimum age in a user's jurisdiction. If we learn that personal information from a child was collected without appropriate consent, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

10. International processing

Information may be processed in countries other than the user's country of residence by us or our service providers, subject to applicable safeguards and laws.

11. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain personal information. You may also choose not to create an account, manage advertising privacy choices where available, or contact us about a privacy request.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Tally, our service providers, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact hello@pada.com.au.
